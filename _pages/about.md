Hi, I am Joong-on, an AI/robotics researcher with a unique interdisciplinary background in Robotics and Medicine. I am currently working as a Principal Researcher at the [Tesollo](https://www.tesollo.com/) R&D Center, leading research on learning-based optimization for multi-joint robot hand control and dexterous manipulation. 

I hold a B.S. in Robotics from Kwangwoon University under the guidance of Prof. [Jin Oh Kim](https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EA%B9%80%EC%A7%84%EC%98%A4_(%EA%B3%B5%ED%95%99%EC%9E%90)) and an M.S. in Medicine from Yonsei University advised by Prof. [Dong-wook Rha](https://medicine.yonsei.ac.kr/medicine/profile-view.do?empNo=eXVoczIwMjBAKUApM9s%2BjFYOFflHVa%2FKDRBLM%2F9e%2Fm3Xjn2ghDa5t3xj1RU%3D), M.D.. Previously, I worked at ETH Zürich (advised by Prof. [William R. Taylor](https://fht.ethz.ch/people/PI/william-taylor.html) and Dr. [Navrag B. Singh](https://fht.ethz.ch/people/PI/navrag-singh.html)) and Severance Hospital, where I focused on deep learning-based human movement analysis and IMU-based wearable feedback. 

By bridging the gap between human biomechanics and robotic kinematics, my goal is to achieve human-level dexterous manipulation in both industrial and academic applications.

**Research Interests:**
* Deep Learning & Reinforcement Learning
* Multi-joint Robot Hand Control & Dexterous Manipulation
* Human Movement Analysis & Biomechanics
