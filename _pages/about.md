Hi, I am Joong-on, an AI/robotics researcher with a unique interdisciplinary background in Robotics and Medicine. I am currently working as a Principal Researcher at the [Tesollo](https://www.tesollo.com/) R&D Center, leading research on learning-based optimization for multi-joint robot hand control and dexterous manipulation. 

I hold a B.S. in Robotics from Kwangwoon University and an M.S. in Medicine from Yonsei University. Previously, I worked at ETH Zürich and Severance Hospital, where I focused on deep learning-based human movement analysis and IMU-based wearable feedback. 

By bridging the gap between human biomechanics and robotic kinematics, my goal is to achieve human-level dexterous manipulation in both industrial and academic applications.

**Research Interests:**
* Deep Learning & Reinforcement Learning
* Multi-joint Robot Hand Control & Dexterous Manipulation
* Human Movement Analysis & Biomechanics
